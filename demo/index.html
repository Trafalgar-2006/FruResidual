Which neurons decide whether the fly sings?

A male fruit fly courts by vibrating one wing to produce a song. The circuit runs brain to muscle: pC1 command neurons → pIP10 descending neuron → TN1/dPR1 nerve-cord premotor → wing motor neurons. This runs that pathway as a spiking simulation on the real connectome, then silences populations to see what breaks.

What silencing breaks

Every condition is compared against 50 random ablations of the same size, matched on total outgoing synapses — because removing busy neurons weakens the song whoever they are. The grey tick marks that matched null. p = 0.020 is the floor at 50 draws and means no matched control came close.

Result

The residual pulls both ways

The fru/dsx-unexplained residual, split by neuron class. Only two classes move the song, and they are the two that connect brain to nerve cord. Bars right of centre weaken the song; bars left of it make it louder.

On that p = 1.000 for cb_intrinsic: the test is one-sided for decreases, so a negative drop scores 1.0 by construction — not because the increase was tested and found wanting. It is left untested rather than claimed.

Why the numbers are trustworthy

Three checks that had to pass first

Orientation

Sensory 4.0, motor 0.01. A transposed matrix leaves node count, edge count, density and E/I ratio identical while inverting every result. Only biology notices: sensory cells must send more than they receive, motor cells the reverse.

Specificity

—The song readout responds to the courtship command this many times more than to size-matched control populations. Turning the gain up gives a louder signal and a worse experiment — at 0.275 the readout cannot tell courtship from noise at all.

Negative control

0.0%490 optic-lobe residual neurons — more than half the entire residual — change the song by exactly nothing. The method is not "remove neurons, output falls".

Limits

What this does not show

  • Not per-neuron dominance. The residual's contribution is collective: per neuron the fru/dsx-explained set is still stronger.
  • Not a courtship mechanism. Wing-motor firing rate is a proxy for "singing". It says nothing about song structure — the pulse and sine patterning the real circuit exists to produce.
  • Not independent of the model. Every number rides on one gain constant, chosen for specificity. Trust the ordering, not the percentages.
  • No synaptic delay or neuromodulation. Transmission is instantaneous between timesteps and monoamines are set to sign zero.
